# Veil product notes

**Register:** product (in-app and dashboard surfaces serve payroll workflows, not marketing chrome).

**Users:** Employer admins running batches, employees checking payslips, auditors reviewing proofs.

**Product purpose:** Demonstrate Midnight's confidential contract model on a payroll-shaped story without pretending the repo is a regulated payroll processor.

**Tone:** Calm, precise, privacy-literate. Say what ships today and what is next.

**Anti-patterns for UI copy:** fake statistics, "revolutionary" filler, hiding the devnet-only warnings.

**Strategic principles:**

1. Ship a green local path before fancy cryptography UX.
2. Every screen names which role it is for.
3. Selective disclosure is the hero beat of the demo, not gradient decorations.
