# Veil product register

**register:** product

## Product purpose

Veil is a privacy-first recruiting surface where candidates keep sensitive career evidence private while recruiters evaluate **Midnight-backed Private Verification**: document-backed **Verified Claims**, candidate-approved **Anonymous Recruiter Views**, and **Disclosure Grants** that upgrade precise claims without exposing raw **Evidence Documents**.

## Users

- **Candidates** need a vault to upload flexible evidence, review coarse vs precise claims, enable anonymous discovery, and approve scoped upgrades per recruiter.
- **Recruiters** need search and match over approved anonymous views and grant flows, never raw files or real identity by default.

## Strategic principles (from CONTEXT)

- Private verification beats matching as the narrative center; matching proves the verification story.
- AI intelligence pipeline is real (extraction, summarization, matching), not seeded mock copy as product behavior.
- Raw evidence stays inside the **Trusted Extraction Boundary**; recruiter surfaces consume claims, views, grants, and receipts only.
- Coarse claims are default-visible; precise claims require disclosure grants.
- Midnight is core infrastructure for commitments and selective disclosure, not decorative branding.

## Anti-references

- Generic “AI recruiting” without disclosure boundaries.
- Legal-grade “verified” or issuer attestation language for MVP claims.
- Culture-fit or protected-trait inference.
- Recruiter-visible raw document text or named profiles in anonymous search.

## Hackathon MVP scope

Demonstrate: evidence → verified claims → anonymous recruiter view → recruiter query/match → disclosure grant with receipts, aligned with **Claim Taxonomy** in CONTEXT.

## Tone

Precise, calm, infrastructure-trust language; short sentences; no hype about replacing humans.
